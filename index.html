I am a student in the Magistère of Fundamental Physics at Paris-Saclay University, currently on an Erasmus+ exchange at the Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität (LMU) in Munich.

Beyond equations, I am a semi-professional breakdancer and a music producer.

MBCHProcess

Wolfram Language package · Research tool · 2026
View repo

Open-source package automating the simplification of complex multi-variable series into generalized hypergeometric functions. Built as part of my IJCLab internship on two-loop sunrise-type Feynman integrals, it is a direct add-on to MBConicHulls.

Wolfram Language Symbolic computing Feynman integrals

Chladni Figures

Numerical simulation · Mathematical physics
View repo

Numerical resolution of the thin plate equation to visualize the nodal lines of vibrational modes of a square plate. Produces the characteristic patterns observed when sand accumulates on a vibrating surface.

C++ Numerical methods PDEs

Lock-exchange flows

Experimental fluid mechanics · w/ Alban Maussang

Experimental and numerical study of gravity currents in a stratified fluid. Compared our measurements to the theoretical predictions of Benjamin (1968) and Shin et al. (2004) on front propagation velocity, current height, and Froude number.
Front tracking by image processing (thresholding + interface detection), front velocity from linear regression on \(x_f(t)\), and objective measurement of the current height via vertical averaging of pixel intensity. Results at \(Re \sim 10^4 - 10^5\).

Python OpenCV (cv2) Fluid mechanics Gravity currents

T.I.P.E. 2024–2025 — Composing the Inaudible

When biological signals become music

Translation of EEG signals into melodies using Pure Data. Designed and assembled a basic EEG acquisition device from scratch (electrodes, amplification, signal conditioning), then sonified the brainwave data in real time.

Pure Data Hardware Signal processing

T.I.P.E. 2023–2024 — Rotation in Breakdance

The headspin movement

Video analysis of the headspin movement using Latis Pro. Modeled the dancer's dynamics: angular momentum conservation, friction at the head-floor interface, and balance control during sustained rotation.

Latis Pro Classical mechanics Video analysis
